# Smaller chin sphere

Reduced radius from **2.411 mm to 2.166 mm**, a **10.17%** reduction. The center moves 1.63 mm toward the tip and 0.16 mm ventrally so the smaller sphere fits the narrower root with no planar cut faces left outside the socket.

All three chin DOFs and their ranges are retained. Controller, actuator gains, fluid settings and saved swimming tuning are unchanged. No optimization was run. Eleven tests passed. All 125 combined rotation samples keep the compiled socket rim covered, with zero measured gap. The original shaft stays rigid and textured.

The model, chin videos 08/09, and Figure 1 PDF/SVG/PNG were regenerated. Other swimming clips retain their earlier renders. Full geometry checks and regeneration commands are in [the socket report](chin_socket.md).
